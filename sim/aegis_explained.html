Aegis in 30 seconds.

More and more businesses let software decide things: a price, a shift, an order. Aegis makes sure each decision follows rules your people wrote, is written down for good, and can explain itself.

What it is

A rulebook, a referee and a record book for automated decisions.

Who it is for

Any business that lets software decide, and needs to answer “who allowed that, and why?”

What it is not

Not another calculator. Aegis does not do the maths, and its AI never decides. It governs whoever does.

Aegis is built to be licensed. Pricing is the first engine we proved it with, and the core is built to govern any other.

In plain English: Aegis is the referee.

Imagine a game. The robot is a very fast player. It works out prices, schedules and orders in a blink. Aegis is the referee, and the referee holds the rulebook.

The robot calculates

It is quick and tireless, but it only knows its numbers. It does not know the rules of your business.

The referee blocks it

If the robot tries to overcharge, Aegis says no and writes it in a book that nobody can erase.

People can change the game

Reality changes, like a holiday weekend. A person can say "this time it is fine", and Aegis writes that down too.

Planned: a second helper, the Ombudsman AI, watches those exceptions. When the same one keeps happening, it learns the pattern and handles it next time, so the rules do not get stuck in the past. roadmap

Three ways automation goes wrong.

Aegis is built to close each one.

The Black Box

A change happens and nobody can say who authorised it, or why.

The Accountability Gap

When an auditor or a customer asks for the record, there is no legal, permanent trail.

The Rogue Engine

An AI optimises hard for one number, with no human context about the business.

Four steps, for any decision.

Each step does one job, and none can do another's.

1 Engine proposes

Pricing, rostering, fleet or stock. An engine works out what it wants to do. It cannot act alone.

›
2 Steward judges

It checks the proposal against the Charter and rules Admit or Deny.

›
3 Seal records

The ruling is sealed in a Warrant, all or nothing. It can never be edited.

›
4 Advocate explains

An AI writes the explanation. Every number is checked, and a fixed template replaces anything that fails.

Pricing is the first engine we built and tested. The platform concept shows the same four steps governing shifts, fleet and stock.

Every role gets its own AI concept

A Resolver Advocate, a Manager Concierge and an Owner Briefing, each limited to what that role may see. Asking for more access, or asking to see another department’s shifts, is itself a request the Steward rules on and records. Today these assistants are scripted in the concept screens.

Departmental Charters

The Owner sets global guardrails, for example no price rise above 40%. Each manager adjusts the limits for their own department inside those guardrails. Nothing waits on the Owner for day-to-day changes.

Earned autonomy

For the first weeks it runs in shadow mode, showing what it would have decided next to what your people did. A type of decision only turns automatic once the match is high and a person switches it on.

Questions.

Is Aegis only for pricing?

No. Aegis governs any decision an engine proposes. Pricing is the first engine we built and tested, and the platform concept shows shifts, fleet and stock under the same rules.

Who is behind Aegis?

A small team building Aegis as licensed software. Pricing was the first engine we proved it with, and the core is built to govern any engine.

Does the AI decide anything?

No. Engines propose and the Steward rules. The AI only writes the explanation afterwards.

Can the rules change themselves?

No. Only a person can change a Charter, and a change opens a new version.

What if the AI is slow or wrong?

Its text is checked, and a fixed template replaces anything that fails. The decision and the Warrant are unaffected.

Can a decision be changed later?

A Warrant can never be edited. A person can override it, and the override is recorded as its own Warrant.
