No record

AI that makes things up

Four steps, every time.

Each step does one job, and none can do another's.

Engine proposes

An engine, such as pricing, works out what it wants to do. It cannot act on its own.

Steward judges

The Steward checks the proposal against the Charter, the rules your people own, and rules Admit or Deny.

Seal records

The decision is sealed in a Warrant. It is all or nothing, and it can never be edited or deleted.

Advocate explains

An AI writes the explanation. Every number in it is checked, and a fixed template replaces anything that fails.

Questions.

Does the AI decide prices?

No. Engines propose and the Steward rules. The AI only writes the explanation afterwards.

Can the rules change themselves?

No. Only a person can change the Charter, and a change opens a new version.

What if the AI is slow or wrong?

The text is checked and a fixed template is used instead. The decision and the Warrant are unaffected.

Can a decision be changed later?

A Warrant cannot be edited. A person can override it, and the override is recorded as its own Warrant.
