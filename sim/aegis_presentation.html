The authority layer for a business.

Engines propose. The Charter decides. Every decision is recorded and can be replayed.

The problem

Smart software can cross lines a business never agreed to.

Prices jump, nobody can say why, and there is no record of who allowed it.

How it works

Three rules we hold

  • People change the rules. The system never does.
  • The AI writes words. It never decides.
  • If a text cannot be checked, a fixed template is used instead.
